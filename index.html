GENERATION I — RED · BLUE · GREEN · YELLOW

All four Gen-1 games in one dex: switch the GAME chips between Pokémon Red, Blue, the Japan-only 1996 Green and Yellow — each with its own theme, its own sprites (Game Boy mono DMG/Pocket + colorized sets, front & back) and its own dex texts, with every entry readable in either the Red/Blue or the Yellow wording. Moves and learnsets follow the selected game, and the Item Dex and the Location Dex (every Kanto location with per-game encounter tables) plus a HOW TO CATCH section on every Pokémon round it off — authentic cries and the same structure as the Emerald dex.
Four game themes in one page Every Gen-1 sprite set + back sprites R/B and Yellow texts, selectable Moves + Item & Location Dex per game

GENERATION II — GOLD · SILVER · CRYSTAL

All three Gen-2 games in one dex, one console generation later: switch the GAME chips between Pokémon Gold, Silver and Crystal — each with its own theme, its own GBC sprites (front & back) and its own dex texts, readable in the Gold, Silver or Crystal wording. Gen 2’s own additions are all here: shiny variants on every set (introduced in this very generation), the series’ first animated battle sprites on the Crystal set, Dark and Steel types with authentic Gen-2 type mechanics, and a Game Boy Color shell for the whole dex — authentic cries and the same structure as the Gen-1 dex.
