    ABOUT

    A fan-made, offline Pokédex covering all 151 Pokémon of Generation I, styled after the in-game Pokédex of Pokémon Yellow. Data, sprites, cries and fonts live in local files next to the page — everything runs without internet.

    ↑ ↓ browse   / search   C cry   P palette   R random   Esc close

    Mark Pokémon as Seen (automatic while browsing) and Owned (pokeball button) to fill your dex status, just like the game. The entry text flips pages like the original — click the text box. Every entry here is the authentic Yellow-version text — Yellow rewrote all 151 entries and gave several Pokémon redrawn sprites. The sprite chips switch between two authentic sprite sets: Y (Game Boy mono, DMG/Pocket palettes) and COLOR (the same Yellow sprites colorized).
